When dependencies/patches/PairingConnectionCtx.java is present, the staged
LibADB JAR excludes the upstream PairingConnectionCtx classes so that the
application build compiles that source replacement. The original AAR is
unchanged. This local patch adds bounded pairing socket setup/read behavior,
cleanup for incomplete setup, and removal of peer-information logging.
The source replacement and its modification notice accompany the bundle.
